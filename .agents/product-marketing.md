# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-11-10

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Sigillum is the private budget app: no account, no bank login, your data stays on your phone.
**What it does:** Android app to record income and expenses (typed, home-screen widget or voice), split income into a 6-category budget ("6 jars" style), track goals, plans and subscriptions, handle several currencies with reference rates, import bank statements (Wise, Revolut, AIB and others) and generate a sealed PDF statement. 8 languages.
**Product category:** Budget planner / expense tracker / money manager (Google Play, Finance).
**Product type:** Mobile app (Capacitor/Android), fully on-device, no server.
**Business model:** Proposed: free forever core + Sigillum Plus subscription (annual €17.99 / R$ 59,90 main, monthly €2.99 / R$ 9,90; 14-day trial on annual; launch offer 40% off year one; lifetime "Founder" Plus for closed-test testers). No ads, no data sales.

## Target Audience
**Primary use case:** Control personal money without handing bank credentials or data to anyone.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Log a spend in seconds, right when it happens
- Know how much is left per budget category this month
- Live between two currencies (earn in EUR, think in BRL)
**Segments (in order):** 1) Brazilians living in Europe (Dublin, Lisbon...); 2) Brazilians in Brazil following the 6 jars method; 3) privacy-conscious people anywhere; 4) expats in general.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Finance apps ask for an account, bank login or sell/analyse data; spreadsheets are slow; multi-currency life is messy.
**Emotional tension:** Distrust of giving bank access; anxiety about not knowing where money went.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Wallet by BudgetBakers, Money Manager, Monefy, Spendee, Mobills, Organizze — most push accounts/bank sync; prices ~US$20–22/yr (Wallet, Money Manager).
**Secondary:** YNAB (US$109/yr, method-heavy, bank sync).
**Indirect:** Spreadsheets, notes app, bank's own app.

## Differentiation
- No account, no server, no bank login; data never leaves the phone
- Quick entry widget and voice
- Multi-currency with rate by entry date
- Statement import without connecting the bank
- Sealed PDF statement the app can verify
- 8 languages; founder is a Brazilian expat in Dublin

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "It doesn't sync with my bank" | Import the statement file in seconds; nobody gets your bank password |
| "If I change phones I lose everything" | Encrypted backup and restore (to build before launch) |
| "Another subscription?" | Core is free forever; Plus only for time-saving extras |

**Anti-persona:** People who want fully automatic bank sync.

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Discreet | Calm, short, confident | Cold or secretive |
| Refined | Lora serif, burgundy wax seal, gold | Luxury snobbery |
| Honest | Says what it does and doesn't | Hype, "best", "#1" |
**Tagline:** "Seu dinheiro. Só seu." / "Your money. Only yours."
**Never use:** "free" in the store title, claims of financial advice, "bank-grade" style hype.

## Goals
**Business goal:** Build a user base first (beachhead: Brazilians in Europe), then convert to Plus.
**Conversion action:** Install → 14-day Plus trial → annual Plus.
**Day-90 targets (hypotheses):** 3,000 installs; 30% store conversion; 20% D30 retention; 4.5★ with 50 reviews.

## Paid Ads (optional)
**Rule:** Only boost organic winners; no ads before 4.5★ and 30 reviews.
**Target CPI:** ≤ €1.00 (Europe), ≤ R$ 2,00 (Brazil). Kill anything 50% over target for 3 days.
**Tracking:** UTM-tagged Play links only; no SDK in the app.
